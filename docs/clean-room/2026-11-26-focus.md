## Setup

- Camera: OBSBOT Tiny 3 Lite, USB `3564:ff04`, firmware `6.5.11.1` (not
  reread).
- Official app: OBSBOT Center `v2.0.17.532` (not reread).
- OS: Windows 10 Pro 19045.
- No AI mode running, preview closed, the camera streaming 1080p60 from the
  last app that opened it.

## Observed: the UI

- The section header shows **Focus**, a round-arrow reset icon after the
  title and a caret on the right. Clicking the caret folds the section.
- **Auto Focus** is a switch. While it is on, the row below is **Auto
  Focus Mode** with a two-part switch **Global | Face**. While it is off,
  that row is replaced by **Manual Focus** with a slider and no number.
- The slider follows dragging; a click on the track did not move it, nor
  did the arrow keys or the mouse wheel.
- Right-clicks showed no menu. Hovering the reset icon showed no tooltip.
- The reset icon acted at once, without a question: Auto Focus on and Face.
  It left the Manual Focus position where it was (turning Auto Focus off
  afterwards showed the slider at the old place).
- After a restart OBSBOT Center showed the state left in the camera: Auto
  Focus off with the slider at the same place, and later Global.

## Observed: USB

Auto Focus and Manual Focus are the standard UVC camera terminal controls
(unit 1), not vendor messages:

- **Auto Focus on:** `SET_CUR` `CT_FOCUS_AUTO_CONTROL` (selector 8), 1 byte
  `01`.
- **Auto Focus off:** selector 8 `00`, then at once
  `CT_FOCUS_ABSOLUTE_CONTROL` (selector 6), 2 bytes, the slider position
  (`00 00` with the slider at the left end, `31 00` later).
- **Manual Focus drag:** selector 6 with the new position about every 60 ms
  while dragging, the last value sometimes twice. Values seen: 0, `53`,
  `64` (100) at the right end, `5a`, `50`, `34`, `31`.
- **Auto Focus Mode:** a framed message on extension unit selector 2,
  command `02 36` to the camera (`02`), flags `25`, data u32: `00 00 00 00`
  Global, `01 00 00 00` Face.
- **Reset:** only what differed: from Auto Focus off in Face, selector 8
  `01`; from Global with Auto Focus on, the Face message. No selector 6
  write.
- On start OBSBOT Center read selector 8 and selector 6 with `GET_CUR`
  (`01`/`00` and `31 00` here).
- The status block (extension unit selector 6) followed every change within
  two seconds:

| offset | meaning | values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | Auto Focus Mode | 0 Global, 1 Face |
| 14 | Auto Focus | 0 off, 1 on |
| 15 | Manual Focus position | 0 to `64` (100); kept while Auto Focus is on |

## Inferred

- The Manual Focus range is 0 to 100; the right end sent 100.
- A higher position focuses nearer. In Gimbly probe snapshots at 10 the
  far wall was sharp; at 90 it was blurred (a person moved in front of
  the camera, so this is not a measurement).
- OBSBOT Center sends the position with Auto Focus off so the camera holds
  the slider's value, not the last automatic one.

## Unknown

- Whether other models use the same unit numbers and status offsets.
- Status values other than those listed; Gimbly treats them as unknown.
- Whether Auto Focus Mode does anything while Auto Focus is off.
- The focus range the camera reports with `GET_MIN`/`GET_MAX`; OBSBOT
  Center did not ask in this capture and Gimbly uses 0 to 100.

## Tests

- `camera::vendor::tests::ai_mode_matches_capture` checks both captured
  Auto Focus Mode messages byte for byte (with their CRCs) and reads
  offsets 13-15, including unknown values and a short block.
- `camera::queue::tests::focus_slider_keeps_only_the_newest_position_in_order`
  checks that slider updates are coalesced without passing switch clicks.
- `camera::control::tests::finds_the_camera_terminal` (macOS) checks the
  descriptor lookup for the camera terminal.

## Checked in Gimbly

2026-11-26, same camera and OS, OBSBOT Center closed: `gimbly --probe
--gimbal DIR af:off focus:10 focus:90 af:global af:on af:face` sent every
step without an error, and the snapshots at positions 10 and 90 differed
in sharpness as above. The next probe process read Auto Focus on, Face,
position 90 from the status block; it turned Auto Focus off, and the probe
after it read off with position 90. Writes go through `IAMCameraControl`
on Windows.
