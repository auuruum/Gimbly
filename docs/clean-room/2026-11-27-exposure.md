## Observed: the UI

- The header shows **Exposure**, a round-arrow reset icon after the title
  and a caret on the right that folds the section. **Anti-Flicker** is a
  separate row below the section and stays visible when it is folded.
- **Auto Exposure** is a switch. While it is on, the section shows **Auto
  Exposure Mode** (Global | Face), **Compensation** (slider and number
  field with arrows) and **ISO Upper and Lower Limit** (one slider with two
  handles, the lower value and "ISO Lower Limit" on its left, the upper
  value and "ISO Upper Limit" on its right).
- While Auto Exposure is off, it shows **Shutter Speed** and **ISO** (each
  a slider and a number field with arrows) and **Exposure Curve** with a
  reset icon and a delete icon, a 4 × 4 dashed grid, a grey diagonal and
  the curve, and the note that the preview or virtual camera shows a
  histogram. Anti-Flicker is greyed out.
- Shutter Speed: 1/60 at the right end, 1/6400 at the left. The up arrow
  makes it faster, the down arrow slower; at 1/60 the down arrow does
  nothing. Steps: 1/60, 1/80, 1/100, 1/120, 1/160, 1/200, 1/240, 1/320,
  1/400, 1/500, 1/640, 1/800, 1/1000, 1/1250, 1/1600, 1/2000, 1/2500,
  1/3200, 1/5000, 1/6400 (no 1/4000). Typed `250` became 1/240.
- ISO: 100 to 6400, arrows step 100. Typed `1234` became 1200; `50` was
  refused.
- Compensation: -3 to 3 in thirds, shown as `-1.3`, `2.7`, `0`. Typed
  `-1.5` became `-1.3`.
- ISO limits snap to 100, 200, 400, 800, 1600, 3200, 6400, 12800. The upper
  handle cannot reach or pass the lower one.
- Exposure Curve: a click on or near the line adds a point there and
  selects it (red lines through it, the delete icon becomes active); a
  click on empty space clears the selection. A selected point is dragged;
  a point dragged outside the graph stays at its edge. The end points do
  not move. Delete removes the selected point, reset makes the line
  straight. Nine points were accepted. Right-click and double-click did
  nothing extra.
- The mouse wheel over sliders and fields changed nothing. No right-click
  menus, no tooltips on the icons.
- Reset icon: Auto Exposure on, Face, compensation 0, ISO limits 100 to
  12800. Anti-Flicker, the manual shutter and ISO stayed.
- After a restart OBSBOT Center showed the state left in the camera,
  including the curve.

## Observed: USB

All vendor messages go to the camera (`02`). Framing as in
[vendor messages](2026-11-08-vendor-messages.md).

- **Auto Exposure off:** `42 24` with u32 `1`, then a read `42 29`, then
  `82 29` with u8 `01` and the u32 shutter from the reply.
- **Auto Exposure on:** `82 29` with u8 `02` and a u32 (`1` from the reset
  and the second capture, `3` once).
- **Read `42 29`:** reply u8 1 (manual) or 2 (auto), then the u32 shutter,
  which the camera keeps while auto.
- **Shutter:** `82 29`, u8 `01`, u32 in units of 100 µs: 1/60 `a7` (167),
  1/80 125, 1/100 100, 1/120 83, 1/160 63, 1/200 50, 1/240 42, 1/320 31,
  1/400 25, 1/500 20, 1/640 16, 1/800 13, 1/1000 10, 1/1250 8, 1/1600 6,
  1/2000 5, 1/2500 4, 1/3200 3, 1/5000 2, 1/6400 1. Sent about every 60 ms
  while dragging, and again when the field lost focus.
- **ISO limits:** `c2 3d`, u32 lower, u32 upper, then 8 bytes that differed
  on every write. The read `82 3d` returns u32 lower, u32 upper, u32 100,
  u32 6400.
- **Auto Exposure Mode:** selector 6 block `03 01 00` Global, `03 01 01`
  Face.
- **Exposure curve:** selector 6 block `26`, length 2 + 2·n, `01`, n, then
  each point's x and y (sorted by x). Examples: `26 04 01 01 7e 7d`, `26 06
  01 02 33 5e 7e cc`, reset `26 02 01 00`. A point dragged out of the
  graph was sent as `f7 ff`. Selector 16 `GET_CUR` returned the stored
  points as `01 01 7e bd` (version, count, points); with no points it
  returned `00 00` or `01 00`.
- **Manual ISO:** UVC processing unit `PU_GAIN_CONTROL` (unit 3, selector 4)
  = ISO / 100 (`40` for 6400, `24` for 3600).
- **Compensation:** `PU_BACKLIGHT_COMPENSATION_CONTROL` (selector 1), 0 to
  `12` (18), `09` for 0.
- **Anti-Flicker:** `PU_POWER_LINE_FREQUENCY_CONTROL` (selector 5), 0 off,
  1 50 Hz, 2 60 Hz.
- **Reset:** `03 01 01`, `82 29 02 …`, the ISO limits when they differed,
  and compensation `09` when it differed.
- On start and wake OBSBOT Center read `42 29`, `82 3d` and the processing
  unit controls with `GET_CUR`.
- Status block (selector 6): offset 3 Anti-Flicker (0, 1, 2), offset 7 Auto
  Exposure Mode (0 Global, 1 Face). Auto Exposure, shutter, ISO,
  compensation and the curve are not in it.

## Inferred

- The shutter unit is 100 µs (10000 / denominator, rounded).
- The 8 extra bytes of the ISO limits write look like uninitialised memory;
  the camera reported 100 and 6400 there regardless, so Gimbly sends those.
- The u32 sent with Auto Exposure on is ignored: the camera kept its stored
  manual shutter.

## Unknown

- What `42 24` means; Gimbly sends it before the manual exposure like the
  official app.
- Whether the shutter list or limits change at 30 fps or on other models.
- The most curve points the camera accepts (9 were seen; Gimbly allows up
  to 28, what one block holds).
- Whether the curve applies while Auto Exposure is on.
- The histogram the official app draws with the preview open; Gimbly does
  not draw one.

## Tests

- `camera::vendor::tests::exposure_matches_capture` checks the captured
  `42 24`, `82 29` and read `42 29` messages byte for byte with their CRCs,
  the replies, the selector 6 blocks for the mode and the curve, the
  selector 16 curve read and status offsets 3 and 7, including bad values.
- `app::view_exposure::tests` check the shutter list mapping and typed
  values (`250` to 1/240), ISO and compensation text, and that the curve
  passes through its points and stays in range.
- `camera::control::tests::finds_the_processing_unit` (macOS) checks the
  descriptor lookup.

## Checked in Gimbly

2026-11-27, same camera and OS, OBSBOT Center closed:

- `gimbly --probe --gimbal DIR wake ae:off shutter:500 iso:800 flicker:60
  ae:global ev:12 curve:64,96 isolimits:200:3200` sent every step without
  an error; the next process read back shutter 1/500, ISO 800, Global, the
  curve and the limits. Compensation and Anti-Flicker written while Auto
  Exposure was off read back unchanged.
- With Auto Exposure on, `ev:12 flicker:60` read back as 12 and 60 Hz in
  the next process: the camera applies both only in auto, matching the
  official UI. Anti-Flicker on Windows uses `IAMVideoProcAmp` property 13.
- `gimbly --probe --virtual-camera --gimbal DIR wake ae:off shutter:60
  shutter:2000 iso:6400 curve:64,200 curve ae:on` saved a frame after every
  step; the frames changed brightness with the shutter and ISO, and the
  curve lifted the shadows.
- In the running app (Image tab): read the camera's state; compensation
  arrow and typed `-2`, ISO limit handles to both ends, Anti-Flicker Off
  and 50 Hz, Global and Face, Auto Exposure off, shutter up arrow, ISO
  drag, curve point added, dragged, deleted, dragged out of the graph
  (stayed in the corner), curve reset, section reset (Face, 0, 100 to
  12800, Anti-Flicker kept), fold. Screenshots of the preview window came
  out black, so frame flow while dragging was checked with the probe above,
  not in the preview.
