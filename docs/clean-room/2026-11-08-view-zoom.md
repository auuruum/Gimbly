# Field of view and zoom

## Source and method

2026-11-08, USBPcap on `\\.\USBPcap3` while selecting the three fields
of view and changing the zoom slider and spin box in OBSBOT Center.
Black-box verification through Gimbly with saved frames. OBSBOT Tiny 3
Lite (`3564:ff04`, USB descriptor revision `0x0510`), Windows 10 Pro
19045. The firmware release and OBSBOT Center version were not recorded.

## Observed

- Field of view uses a 60-byte SET_CUR on selector 6 of extension unit 2
  (interface 0). Bytes 0–2 are `04 01 XX` and the rest are zero.
  `XX` is `00` for Wide, `01` for Medium and `02` for Narrow.
- Manual zoom uses selector 2, address `02`, command `42 19`, flags
  `25`. The payload starts with a little-endian u32 value of 8,
  followed by the ratio multiplied by 100 as another u32. Both input
  controls used the same command.
- Command `04 68` to address `04` reads the zoom as a little-endian f32.
  After setting 2.5× in Gimbly, the preset save read 2.5× from that
  command and the camera stored 250 in the preset. Gimbly frames show
  the three view widths and manual zoom at 1×, 2.5× and 4×. With time
  to settle, the three FOV selections read 1.00×, 1.18× and 1.42×.

## Unknown

The selector 6 status reads in the FOV capture did not identify which
button was selected. A method to read the selected FOV independently of
the zoom ratio is unknown. Gimbly shows the last selected FOV during
the session and clears it on manual zoom or preset recall.

## Test

`camera::vendor::tests::view_commands_match_capture` checks the FOV
blocks, zoom encoding and zoom readback. Hardware test with
`gimbly --probe --gimbal DIR wide medium narrow zoom:2.5 zoom:4 zoom:1`.
