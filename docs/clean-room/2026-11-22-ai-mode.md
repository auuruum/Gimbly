## Setup

- Camera: OBSBOT Tiny 3 Lite, USB `3564:ff04`, firmware `6.5.11.1`.
- Official app: OBSBOT Center `v2.0.15.186`.
- OS: Windows 10 Pro 19045.
- Human Tracking off at the start, Sound Source Assisted Tracking on.

## Observed

All writes below are SET_CUR on extension unit selector 6 (60 bytes, rest
zero) or vendor messages on selector 2 (see
[vendor messages](2026-11-08-vendor-messages.md)) to the gimbal (`0x04`).
"Offset N" is a byte of the selector 6 status block the app reads about
once a second.

### Voice Tracking and Hand Tracking

- **Voice Tracking on:** selector 6 `16 02 07 00`. Offset 24 read `07` and
  offset 28 read `02` 2 s later.
- **Hand Tracking on:** `16 02 03 00`. Offset 24 read `03`, offset 28 `00`.
- **Off** (click on the highlighted button): `16 02 00 00`. Offset 24 read
  `00`.
- **Switching** between the two sends only the start of the other mode
  (`16 02 07 00` or `16 02 03 00`), no stop first.
- Offset 24 sometimes read `06` for under 2 s between `03` and `07`.
- While Hand Tracking runs the app greys out the Zone Tracking switch and
  shows it off.
- The two buttons act as one choice: at most one is highlighted.

### Voice Tracking Range

Shown under the buttons only while Voice Tracking runs: a two-part switch
labelled Omnidirectional and Forward.

- **Forward:** selector 6 `24 01 01`. Offset 46 changed `01` → `03`.
- **Omnidirectional:** `24 01 00`. Offset 46 changed `03` → `01`.
- Offset 46 bit 0 followed Sound Source Assisted Tracking in earlier
  sessions; it stayed `1` here.

### Left Hand / Right Hand

Shown while Hand Tracking runs, with a **Set** button on the "Hand
Tracking" row above them.

- **Right Hand:** gimbal message `44 20`, data `u32 4`, `u8 0`.
- **Left Hand:** the same with `u8 1`.
- After waking the camera the app read it with `84 20`, data `u32 4`. The
  reply was the single byte `01` (Left Hand highlighted).

### Hand Tracking Set panel

Clicking Set replaces the console with a panel: Cancel and Done at the top,
a range box with four limits (shown 30.0°, −30.0°, 45.0°, −45.0°), a line
"P: 0.00° | T: 0.00° | Z: 1.00" for the position the gimbal returns to when
the target leaves the range, then a joystick, a zoom slider and Wide /
Medium / Narrow. Right-clicking a limit or the P/T/Z line shows one entry,
**Update**.

Opening (29:26, 31:52):

1. `04 25` with data `ff`, then `04 25` with no data. Both replies:
   `ff ff ff ff`, then `i16` `0, 0, 0, 100`, then `0, 0, 100, 100, 100, 0`.
2. `84 20` with `u32` 0, 1, 2, 3. Replies `f32` −45, 45, −30, 30.
3. `84 20` with `u32` 0–3 and one more byte `00`. Same replies.
4. Selector 6 `18 01 01`.
5. Gimbal `c4 26` with data `00`.

**Done** with nothing changed (29:33):

1. Selector 6 `18 01 00`.
2. `44 20` four times: `u32` key 0–3, then the `f32` from step 2 (−45, 45,
   −30, 30).
3. `c4 24` with the 24 bytes read in step 1, except that the `i16` at
   byte 20 (`100`) was written as `0`.
4. `c4 26` with `01`.
5. `84 68` with `u32 0x1e`, `f32` 1.0.

**Cancel** (29:48, 32:00): only steps 1, 4 and 5 of Done.

Matching the panel to the values: key 0 = −45 is shown as the right limit
"−45.0°", key 1 = 45 as the left limit "45.0°", key 2 = −30 as the top
"30.0°", key 3 = 30 as the bottom "−30.0°".

### The camera changes the mode by itself

- After Resume at 30:13 (`c2 a0` with `u32 0` to address `0x02`) offset 24
  changed `03` (Hand) → `07` (Voice) at 30:18 with no write from the app.
- After a Hand Tracking click at 30:36 (`07` → `06` → `03`), offset 24 went
  back to `07` at 30:51, again with no write.
- After Cancel at 32:00 it went `03` → `06` → `07` within 3.5 s.
- From 28:27 to 28:49 and from 29:19 to 29:45 Hand Tracking stayed on.
- The app followed offset 24: it highlighted Voice Tracking and showed the
  range row each time.

### Sleep

The camera went to sleep twice with no click (29:45 and 32:15, offset 30
`55` → `00`), about 2 min after the app's preview stopped. That matches the
2 min Auto Sleep setting in the More tab.

## Inferred

- Selector 6 command `16 02 NN` starts AI mode `NN` and `00` stops it.
  `02` is Human Tracking (earlier notes), `03` Hand Tracking, `07` Voice
  Tracking. Offset 24 holds the running mode, so only one runs at a time.
  `06` looks like a short switching state.
- `24 01 NN` sets the Voice Tracking range; bit 1 of offset 46 reports
  Forward.
- Gimbal group `44 20` / `84 20` holds Hand Tracking settings: keys 0–3
  the range in degrees as `f32` in the camera's sign (yaw min, yaw max,
  pitch min, pitch max), key 4 the hand (`1` left, `0` right). The panel
  shows pan as is and tilt negated.
- `04 25` / `c4 24` read and write the return position as the 24-byte
  record used for presets (`u32` id, `i16` yaw ×100, pitch ×100, roll, zoom
  ×100, six more `i16`). Id `ff ff ff ff` means none stored.
- `18 01 01` and `c4 26 00` put the camera in a range-edit state while the
  panel is open; `18 01 00` and `c4 26 01` end it. `84 68` with `0x1e` sets
  the zoom back to 1.0×.
- The camera may switch from Hand to Voice by itself; a client has to keep
  reading offset 24 rather than trust its own last write.

## Unknown

- Why the camera switches Hand → Voice. It happened after a wake and after
  leaving the Set panel, not every time.
- Offset 28 (`02` only during Voice Tracking).
- The `i16` at byte 20 of the return record (`100` read, `0` written).
- Why the app reads the limits twice (with and without the extra byte).
- What Update writes; it was not clicked. We infer that it takes the
  current gimbal angle and zoom into the panel, and Done writes them.
