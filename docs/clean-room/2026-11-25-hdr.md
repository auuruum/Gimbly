# HDR

## Source and method

2026-11-25. USB capture with USBPcap (snap length 512, device address 1 on
the camera's root hub `\.\USBPcap1`, descriptors injected), Gimbly closed,
OBSBOT Center the only app using the camera. Claude Code clicked the HDR
switch at the top of the **Image** tab of the visible OBSBOT Center UI and
changed **Software Settings → Output → Resolution** in between, checking
the UI after each click. The capture stays outside the repository and was
decoded with our own script.

| time | click |
| --- | --- |
| 19:46:52 | HDR on (Resolution `1080P 60`) |
| 19:47:04 | HDR off |
| 19:47:34 | Resolution `4K 30` |
| 19:47:44 | HDR on |
| 19:47:55 | Resolution `1080P 60`, HDR left on |
| 19:48:02 | HDR off |

## Setup

- Camera: OBSBOT Tiny 3 Lite, USB `3564:ff04`, firmware `6.5.11.1` (not
  reread).
- Official app: OBSBOT Center `v2.0.17.532`.
- OS: Windows 10 Pro 19045.
- No AI mode running.

## Observed

- **On:** one SET_CUR on extension unit selector 6: `01 01 01` (rest of the
  60 bytes zero). Status offset 6 changed `00` → `01` within 0.4 s.
- **Off:** selector 6 `01 01 00`. Offset 6 changed `01` → `00`.
- The same bytes came at `1080P 60` and at `4K 30`.
- Changing the Resolution sent no selector 2 or selector 6 write. The HDR
  switch kept its state across the change, and the next status read still
  had offset 6 at `01`.
- The UI shows HDR as a switch with a help icon. Hovering or clicking the
  help icon showed nothing in this session.

## Observed: HDR and the frame rate

After Gimbly had streamed 1080p60, OBSBOT Center showed the HDR switch
greyed out, also after a restart and with its own Resolution set to
`1080P 60` or `4K 30`. OBSBOT Center does not open the video itself while
its preview is closed, so the camera keeps the mode the last app used.

Comparing the startup reads of both sessions, only status offsets 30 and 31
differed: `00 1e` in the first session, `55 3c` in the greyed one. A second
capture (19:57-19:58, Gimbly probe streaming each mode for about 8 s, no
other app open):

| mode | offset 30 | offset 31 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1080p60 MJPEG | `55` | `3c` (60) |
| 4K30 NV12 | `00` | `1e` (30) |
| 1080p30 NV12 | `00` | `1e` (30) |
| 1080p60 MJPEG | `55` | `3c` (60) |

OBSBOT Center opened after the 1080p30 stream showed HDR enabled; opened
after the last 1080p60 stream it showed HDR greyed. Offset 6 stayed `01`
(HDR on) across all modes; the camera did not clear it.

## Inferred

- Offset 31 is the frame rate the camera streams at.
- The official app allows HDR only at 30 fps (4K 30 and 1080P 30 here), not
  at 60 fps. The owner suggested "HDR works only in 4K"; 1080P 30 also
  enabled it.
- The Resolution setting in the official app does not reach the camera; the
  stream format of the last app that opened the video decides.

## Unknown

- Whether the camera keeps HDR after a power cycle.
- Whether HDR changes the frame rate the camera delivers at 60 fps; frame
  timing was not measured.
- Offset 6 values other than 0 and 1; Gimbly treats them as unknown.
- What HDR does in the picture at 60 fps, if anything, when it stays on.
- The meaning of offset 30 (`55` at 1080p60). 720p30, 720p60 and 120 fps
  modes were not tried.
